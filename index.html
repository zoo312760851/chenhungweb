FEATURED / MATERIALS RESEARCHONGOING

GaAsBi/GaAs(111)B Epitaxy & Crystal-Structure Analysis

研究重點包含攣晶形成、晶體取向、局部結構變形，以及 HRXRD 與 RSM 訊號的跨量測對應。核心目標不是只找到峰值，而是說明每個訊號如何產生、它支持哪一個模型，又該如何被下一個實驗驗證。

The project investigates twin formation, crystal orientation, local structural distortion, and cross-mapping between HRXRD and RSM. The goal is not merely to identify peaks, but to explain how each signal arises and how the model can be tested.
