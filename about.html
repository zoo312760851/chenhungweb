About Me

跨領域不是同時列出很多工具，而是理解不同系統之間的介面，並把它們整合成可驗證的成果。

Interdisciplinary work is not a list of tools—it is understanding interfaces between systems and integrating them into results that can be validated.

機械工程給我嚴謹的數理、設計與系統分析基礎；電子領域的學習，則讓我更深入理解訊號、元件與系統整合。奈米與固態相關課程，也使我能從材料與元件尺度思考工程問題。

Mechanical engineering gives me a rigorous foundation in mathematics, design, and systems analysis; electronics deepens my understanding of signals, devices, and integration. Coursework in nanotechnology and solid-state topics also enables me to reason from material and device scales.

我在意的不只是「做出來」，也在意結果能否被量測、解釋與改善。面對陌生任務，我會先拆解問題、確認介面與限制，再透過模型、實驗與討論逐步收斂。

I care not only about making something work, but also whether it can be measured, explained, and improved. When facing unfamiliar tasks, I break down the problem, identify interfaces and constraints, and converge through modeling, experiments, and discussion.
