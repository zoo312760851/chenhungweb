01 / AFM SYSTEM DEVELOPMENT

奈米精度 Flexure Guide 與量測驗證

Nano-precision flexure guide and measurement validation

參與原子力顯微鏡系統的機構設計與驗證。以 CAD / FEA 評估位移、剛性、共振與耦合，再利用雷射位移量測與掃頻實驗比對模型。

Contributed to the mechanical design and validation of an AFM system. CAD / FEA were used to evaluate travel, stiffness, resonance, and coupling, followed by laser displacement and frequency-sweep experiments.

01第一代設計占用空間過大，重新設計橋式＋槓桿型撓性結構。Redesigned the initial oversized concept into a bridge-and-lever flexure mechanism.
02以 FEA 模擬最大位移量與前兩階共振頻率。Simulated maximum travel and the first two resonance modes with FEA.
03以雷射位移計與掃頻量測驗證模型。Validated the model using laser displacement and frequency-sweep measurements.

02 / FPGA & GUI

Encoder 訊號處理與 Qt/C++ GUI

Encoder signal processing and Qt/C++ GUI

以 Verilog 在 FPGA 上讀取 A+、A−、B+、B− 四組 Encoder 訊號，利用反相信號檢查提升可靠性，並以 Qt/C++ 建立 UART 雙向通訊介面。

Used Verilog on an FPGA to read A+, A−, B+, and B− encoder signals, adding complementary-signal checks for reliability and building a bidirectional UART interface with Qt/C++.

01辨識 Encoder 數位訊號與方向。Decode encoder digital signals and direction.
02利用 A−、B− 反相信號進行錯誤檢查。Use complementary A− and B− signals for error checks.
03透過 UART 傳輸多位元資料與開關狀態。Exchange multi-bit data and switch states over UART.
