AFM SYSTEM DEVELOPMENT

原子力顯微鏡系統：從撓性機構到 FPGA 控制

AFM system: from flexure mechanics to FPGA control

在中研院研究實習中，參與奈米精度平台的機構、量測、控制與介面整合。核心價值不是單一元件，而是讓機械、電子與軟體真正協同運作。

During my Academia Sinica internship, I worked across precision mechanics, measurement, control, and interface integration. The key contribution was making mechanical, electronic, and software layers work together.

01以 CAD 與 FEA 設計具位移放大機制的 Flexure Guide，評估位移、剛性、共振與耦合。Designed a displacement-amplifying flexure guide with CAD and FEA, evaluating travel, stiffness, resonance, and axis coupling.
02使用雷射位移量測與掃頻實驗，比對理論模型與實際響應。Validated the model through laser displacement measurement and frequency sweep experiments.
03以 Verilog / FPGA 讀取 Encoder 訊號，並透過 Qt/C++ 與 UART 建立 GUI 通訊測試。Read encoder signals with Verilog/FPGA and built Qt/C++ GUI communication tests over UART.
